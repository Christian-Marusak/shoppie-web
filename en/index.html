What Shoppie does

  • 🛒

    Quick adding

    Tap + to add an item. Press and hold + to open a wheel with your favourite items.

  • ✨

    Knows what you're running low on

    Shoppie learns from your completed shopping trips and suggests what you probably need.

  • 🔁

    Recurring items

    Coffee every 2 weeks, toilet paper every month. They add themselves to your list.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧

    Shared household

    Share your shopping with your family via iCloud. Everyone sees the changes.

  • 🧾

    Receipt scanning

    Snap a receipt or pick one from your photos. It's read right on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

  • 🥛

    Best before

    Best-before dates and morning reminders, so nothing goes to waste.

Plus shopping history and optional Warranties.
