What Shoppie does

  • 🛒

    Quick adding

    Tap + and type what you need. Shoppie knows about 540 common products and sorts them into categories for you. You can add a quantity too.

  • 🎡

    Favourites wheel

    Press and hold + and pick what you buy most often from the wheel. You choose what the wheel offers.

  • ✨

    Knows what you’re running low on

    Shoppie learns from your shopping and suggests what you probably need.

  • 🧾

    Receipt scanning

    Snap a receipt and Shoppie recognises products and prices, including discounts. Right on your iPhone, even offline.

  • 🥛

    Pantry

    Food you buy shows up on its own with a best-before date. Each morning Shoppie reminds you what to eat soon, and it can remove expired food for you.

  • 🔁

    Recurring items

    Coffee every 2 weeks, toilet paper every month. They’re added to your list on the day you choose.

  • 🛡️

    Warranties

    All your warranties in one place, with a photo of the receipt. You get a reminder before a warranty ends.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧

    Shared household

    Invite your family via iCloud. Everyone sees changes to your lists right away.

  • 🕘

    History and multiple lists

    Every shopping trip with the store and number of items. Swipe right to add an item again. Keep as many lists as you need.

Designed for iPhone Duo too

Keep scrolling to unfold it. Shoppie moves from the outer display to the large inner display.

On an unfolded iPhone Duo, Shoppie uses the entire large inner display.

What’s coming

Shoppie keeps getting better. This is our path – the further it goes, the more it fades into the mist.

  1. Available now

    • ✅

      Version 1.0

      A shared shopping list for the whole household with quick add, suggestions based on your shopping, receipt scanning, Pantry and warranties.

  2. Coming next

    • 📱

      Home Screen widget

      See your shopping list and tick off items right from your iPhone’s Home Screen, without opening the app.

    • 📝

      Item notes

      Add a note to an item, such as which brand or flavour to buy, so there are no mix-ups in the shop.

    • 📊

      Shopping overview

      See what you buy, how often, and how much you’ve spent, based on your scanned receipts.

  3. Later

    • 🛒

      In-store mode

      When you arrive at the shop, your list adapts for shopping and sorts items in the order you walk through the store.

    • 🔒

      Shopping from the Lock Screen

      See and tick off the remaining items without unlocking your phone.

    • 💶

      Prices and budget

      Find out roughly what your shopping will cost and how well you’re keeping to your monthly food budget.

  4. …and whatever you suggest

    Have an idea? Write to us.
