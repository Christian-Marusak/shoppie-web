Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Developer: Christián Marušák

In short: Shoppie collects nothing about you. Your data lives on your iPhone and in your iCloud, not with me.

Summary

  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No third-party SDKs or services.
  • No sign-up and no login.
  • As the developer, I have no access to your data.

What data I collect

None. Shoppie does not send the developer any data about you or about how you use the app.

Where your data is stored

Everything in the app – lists, items, shopping history, best-before items, recurring items, warranties and settings – is stored on your device and synced through your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit).

This data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Household sharing

If you invite someone to your household, invited members can see and edit the household's shared data: lists, history, best-before items and recurring items.

Warranties and settings are not shared.

Sharing works through iCloud. A member can leave the household at any time, and the owner can remove members.

Camera and photos

The camera and photos are used only to scan receipts. The text is read right on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence. The photo is never sent anywhere or stored – only the recognised items and prices are saved to your history.

Notifications

Best-before reminders are local – they are scheduled on your iPhone. I don't send any push notifications.

Deleting your data

  • In the app's Settings: Delete Shopping History and Delete Best Before.
  • By deleting individual items in the app.
  • By removing the app and its data from iCloud: iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Children

Shoppie is not directed specifically at children and collects no data about anyone, including children.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email me at christianm@outlook.sk.
