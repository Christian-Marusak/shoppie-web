Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026 · Developer: Christián Marušák

In short: Shoppie collects nothing about you. Your data lives on your iPhone and in your iCloud, not with us.

Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no login.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
  • No third-party SDKs or services.
  • No server – the app sends us no data at all.
  • We have no access to your data.

What data we collect

None. Shoppie does not send us any data about you or about how you use the app. We have no server that data could be sent to.

That is why Shoppie is labelled “Data Not Collected” on the App Store.

Where your data is stored

Everything in the app – lists, shopping history, Pantry, recurring items, warranties, receipt photos and settings – is stored on your device and synced between your devices through your private iCloud (Apple CloudKit).

We have no access to this data. It is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Household sharing

If you invite someone to your household via iCloud, household members can see and edit lists, shopping history, Pantry and recurring items.

Warranties (including their receipt photos) and settings stay private. The one exception is the Pantry auto-remove setting, which applies to the whole household.

The owner can stop sharing or remove an individual member at any time. A member can leave the household at any time.

Camera and photos

The camera and your photo library are used for two things only: scanning receipts and adding a receipt photo to a warranty.

The text is read right on your iPhone using Apple Vision. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, it is additionally processed by Apple’s on-device model. Nothing is sent to the internet.

A receipt photo added to a warranty stays only on your device and in your private iCloud and is never sent anywhere.

Notifications

Best-before and warranty-expiry reminders are local – they are scheduled on your iPhone itself. We don’t send any push notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in iPhone Settings.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: delete your shopping history or Pantry in the app’s settings; delete individual items and warranties directly in the app.
  • In iCloud: iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Shoppie.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone removes the data stored on the device. To remove the copy in iCloud, use Manage Storage (see above).

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or delete – you are in full control of your data.

Children

Shoppie is not directed specifically at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no external fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the effective date at the top.
