Receipt scanner for iPhone: items and prices in seconds

Updated 27 November 2026

In short

Shoppie's receipt scanner turns a paper receipt into a list of items with prices. Just take a photo: Shoppie recognises the items and discounts, saves the trip to History and moves food into Pantry. It all happens on your iPhone, even offline.

Paper receipts get lost and fade. Shoppie's receipt scanner turns them into a clear record in seconds: what you bought, how much it cost and where. And because the scan recognises food, it also helps you keep track of what needs eating.

What the receipt scanner does

  • Recognises items and prices, including discounts printed on the receipt.
  • Reads the store, date and total.
  • Saves the trip to History, so you can find it any time.
  • Moves food into Pantry with an estimated best-before date.
  • If you like, clears your shopping list by removing the items you bought.

How it works

  1. On the List tab, tap the Scan receipt icon.
  2. Take a photo of the receipt or choose one from your photos.
  3. Check the items – rename one, change its category or leave it out.
  4. Tap Save. Done.

For a step-by-step version with screenshots, see How to scan a receipt on iPhone.

Why scanning receipts pays off

One scan saves you work in several places. Food shows up in Pantry without any typing, and Shoppie reminds you in the morning what to eat before it goes off. In History you can quickly see when you bought something, and swiping an item to the right adds it back to your list. Shoppie also learns from finished trips what you are running low on.

Privacy: the receipt never leaves your iPhone

Text is read on your iPhone using Apple Vision, so scanning works on any iPhone with iOS 26, even without a connection. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model also helps with product names. The photo is never uploaded, and Shoppie has no server and no accounts.

Tips for the best result

  • Flatten the receipt and capture all of it.
  • Use good light and keep the photo sharp.
  • Scan soon after shopping – thermal paper fades.
  • Before saving, rename abbreviations into clear names.

Frequently asked questions

Does the receipt scanner work offline?

Yes. The receipt is read on your iPhone and nothing is sent anywhere, so it even works in Airplane Mode.

Does Shoppie recognise discounts on the receipt?

Yes, the scan picks up items and prices, including discounts printed on the receipt.

Where is a scanned trip saved?

In your shopping History. Food also appears in Pantry with an estimated best-before date.

What if the scan gets something wrong?

Before saving you can rename an item, change its category or leave it out. A sharp photo of the whole receipt in good light helps most.
