The iPhone shopping list

Features

Everything Shoppie can do, in one place. Each feature links to step-by-step guides.

  • Shopping list

    Shoppie is a free shopping list app for iPhone. It knows about 540 products and sorts them into categories for you. No ads, no sign-up.

    Learn more →

  • Shared household

    A shared shopping list in Shoppie: invite your family to a household via iCloud. Lists, history and Pantry update for everyone straight away.

    Learn more →

  • Receipt scanning

    Shoppie scans your grocery receipt and recognises items and prices, discounts included. On your iPhone, offline, with nothing uploaded.

    Learn more →

  • Pantry

    Shoppie logs the food you buy with an estimated best-before date and reminds you each morning what to eat first. Less food in the bin.

    Learn more →

  • Warranties

    Shoppie saves each warranty with a photo of the receipt and reminds you a month before it ends, while you can still claim. Private, on iPhone.

    Learn more →

  • Suggestions and wheel

    Shoppie learns from your shopping: it suggests what you're running low on, and the favourites wheel adds frequent items in one move. On your iPhone.

    Learn more →

  • Regular items

    Coffee every 2 weeks, nappies every month: set up a recurring shopping list in Shoppie once, and items appear on your list on the day you choose.

    Learn more →

  • Lists and history

    Keep multiple shopping lists on your iPhone – groceries, toiletries, Christmas. Shoppie saves finished trips to History with the store and item count.

    Learn more →

  • Privacy

    Shoppie is a private shopping list app with no sign-up, ads or tracking. No server, no data collected – everything stays on your iPhone and in iCloud.

    Learn more →

  • Accessibility and languages

    An accessible shopping list for iPhone: Shoppie supports VoiceOver, larger text, Dark Mode, landscape and iPhone Duo – in English, Slovak and Czech.

    Learn more →
