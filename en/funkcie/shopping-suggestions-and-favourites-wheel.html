Shopping suggestions and favourites wheel: a list that learns

Updated 27 November 2026

In short

Shopping suggestions in Shoppie show you what you are probably running low on. Shoppie learns from your finished trips and shows suggestions at the top of your list. Touch and hold + to open the favourites wheel and add frequent items with one slide of your finger.

You buy most things again and again: milk, bread, coffee, bread rolls. So Shoppie doesn't wait for you to remember them. It learns from your finished shopping trips what you buy and how often, and helps you in two ways – suggestions and the favourites wheel.

Suggestions: what you're running low on

When it's about time you usually buy something, Shoppie shows it at the top of your list under “You might need”. There's nothing to set up – just shop as usual and finish each trip. After a few trips, suggestions start to appear on their own.

  • Tap + next to an item to add just that one.
  • Tap Add All to add every suggestion at once.

The guide How Shoppie suggests what you're running out of explains it in detail.

Favourites wheel: add in one move

For the things you buy most often, there's no need to type. Touch and hold the green + button to open the favourites wheel, and add an item without touching the keyboard.

How it works

  1. Instead of tapping, touch and hold the green + button. A wheel of bubbles appears around it.
  2. Without lifting your finger, slide to an item bubble, for example bread rolls.
  3. Let go. The item is on your list straight away.

For screenshots, see How to add items fast with the favourites wheel.

Choose what the wheel offers

In Settings → List → Quick add wheel, pick what the wheel shows:

  • Suggested – what Shoppie currently recommends.
  • Recently bought – what you bought last.
  • Most frequent – the things you buy most.

The wheel appears after your first finished trip, because it learns from your History.

Suggestions, the wheel or regular items?

All three save you typing, but each suits a different situation:

  • Suggestions – when you don't want to forget anything and are happy to let Shoppie guess what is running low.
  • Favourites wheel – when you know what you want and need to add it as fast as possible, even in the shop.
  • Regular items – when you buy something on the same day every time, say every Friday. They add themselves to your list on the day you choose.

It learns on your iPhone

Suggestions and the wheel are worked out on your iPhone. Your shopping is never sent anywhere, Shoppie has no server and collects nothing about you. The more trips you finish, the better the suggestions get.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't Shoppie suggest anything yet?

Suggestions need a few finished shopping trips. Shop as usual and finish each trip – suggestions will start to appear.

Why don't I see the favourites wheel?

The wheel learns from your shopping, so it appears once there's at least one finished trip in History.

Can I change what the wheel offers?

Yes. In Settings → List → Quick add wheel, choose Suggested, Recently bought or Most frequent.

Does Shoppie send my shopping to a server?

No. Suggestions are calculated on your iPhone and nothing is sent anywhere.
