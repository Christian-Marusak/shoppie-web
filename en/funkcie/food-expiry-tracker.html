Food expiry tracker app for iPhone: Shoppie Pantry

Updated 27 November 2026

In short

Shoppie's Pantry is a food expiry tracker app that fills itself: food you buy appears with an estimated best-before date you can edit. Each morning Shoppie reminds you what to eat first, and if you like, it removes food past its date on its own.

The yoghurt at the back of the fridge, the cheese you forgot about, the bread that went stale. Pantry in Shoppie helps you see what you have at home and what needs eating first – without typing everything in or keeping a spreadsheet.

What Pantry does

  • Food shows up on its own when you finish a shopping trip or scan a receipt.
  • Estimates a best-before date, which you can change to match the pack at any time.
  • The All / Soon / Expired / Fresh tiles show what is due and also work as filters.
  • Reminds you in the morning what to eat soon.
  • If you want, it removes food past its date automatically – this is off by default.

How it works

  1. Tap the Pantry tab.
  2. Tap a food to open its details with the Use by date. Fix any date marked “estimate” to match the pack.
  3. When you finish something, swipe right → Used Up. If it has to go, swipe left → Thrown Away.
  4. In Settings → Notifications, turn on Use-by reminders.

For a step-by-step version with screenshots, see How to track food best-before dates on iPhone.

A morning reminder of what to eat

Shoppie sends one notification in the morning listing the food to eat first. In Remind me, choose On the day or 1, 2, 3 or 7 days before; in Time, pick an hour between 6:00 and 21:00. Your iPhone schedules the reminders itself – we don't send any push notifications from a server. More in How to set a morning reminder.

Auto-remove food past its date

Rather not clear out expired food by hand? In Settings → Pantry → Remove from Pantry, change Never to after 1, 2, 3 or 7 days, then use Count as to choose Used Up or Thrown Away. The setting applies to the whole household and works with estimated dates too. See How to turn on auto-remove.

One Pantry for the whole household

If you share a household through iCloud, every member sees the same Pantry. When your partner marks the milk as used up, you see it straight away. Everything stays on your iPhones and in your private iCloud.

Tips for wasting less food

  • Check the estimated date against the pack after shopping – the date on the package is what counts.
  • Set the reminder a day or two early so you have time to plan a meal.
  • Glance at the Soon tile before you shop, so you don't buy what you already have.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to add food to Pantry by hand?

No. Food appears in Pantry on its own when you finish a trip or scan a receipt, with an estimated best-before date.

Can I change the estimated date?

Yes. Tap the food and edit the Use by date in its details. The date on the package is what counts.

Does Shoppie remove expired food automatically?

Only if you turn it on in Settings → Pantry → Remove from Pantry. The default is Never.

Can my family see the Pantry?

Yes. Pantry is shared with your household through iCloud, so everyone sees what needs eating.
