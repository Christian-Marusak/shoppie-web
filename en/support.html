Frequently asked questions

Shopping list

How do I add an item?

Tap the green + button at the bottom and type what you want to buy. Shoppie knows common products (a catalogue of about 540 items), so it picks the category for you.

You can also add a quantity.

How does the quick-add wheel work?

Press and hold + to open a wheel with the things you buy most often. Pick one and it’s on your list.

You can choose what the wheel offers.

Can I have more than one list?

Yes. Tap the list name at the top to switch between your lists.

What happens when I complete a shopping trip?

Checked-off items are saved to History and removed from the list.

History shows all your trips with the store and number of items. Swipe an item to the right to add it back to your list.

Why doesn’t Shoppie suggest anything?

Suggestions come from your completed shopping trips. Shoppie needs a few of them to learn what you buy and how often.

Just shop as usual and complete each trip at the end. Suggestions will start to appear on their own.

How do recurring items work?

For things you buy regularly (say, coffee every 2 weeks), set how often you buy them. Shoppie adds them to your list automatically on the day you choose.

Receipt scanning

How does receipt scanning work?

Take a photo of a receipt and Shoppie recognises the products and prices, including discounts.

The text is read right on your iPhone using Apple Vision, so scanning works on any iPhone with iOS 26, even offline. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple’s on-device model additionally processes it. Nothing is sent to the internet.

The scan didn’t recognise everything. What can I do?

It helps if:

  • the receipt is flat and fully in the frame,
  • there’s enough light and the photo isn’t blurry,
  • the receipt isn’t faded or crumpled.

Check the items before saving and correct them if needed.

The scanner won’t start or I can’t see the camera

Shoppie needs camera access. Allow it in iPhone Settings → Apps → Shoppie → Camera.

Pantry and reminders

How does Pantry work?

Food you buy appears in Pantry automatically, with an estimated best-before date or one you enter.

When you finish something, mark it as eaten; if you have to throw it away, mark it as thrown away.

What is auto-remove?

Food past its best-before date can be removed from Pantry automatically after 1, 2, 3 or 7 days. You choose whether it counts as Used Up or Thrown Away.

It is off by default. The setting applies to the whole household, so to all members.

Why am I not getting reminders?

Shoppie sends one notification in the morning listing food you should eat soon, plus a reminder before a warranty ends. Notifications are scheduled on your iPhone itself.

If they don’t arrive:

  • allow notifications: iPhone Settings → Apps → Shoppie → Notifications,
  • in the app’s settings, check when you want to be reminded (on the day or a few days before),
  • make sure the food has a best-before date,
  • and check that a Focus mode isn’t hiding notifications.

Warranties

How do warranties work?

Save a warranty for anything you buy, together with a photo of the receipt. Shoppie reminds you before the warranty ends.

In the EU, new goods come with a warranty of at least 24 months.

Warranties are private – they are not shared with your household. If you don’t use them, you can hide the Warranties tab in settings.

Household and sync

How do I invite my family, and what is shared?

You send the household invitation right from the app. Sharing works through iCloud, so the person you invite needs an iPhone signed in to an Apple Account with iCloud turned on.

Household members can see and edit lists, shopping history, Pantry and recurring items. You can also see the list of members in the app.

Warranties and settings are not shared (except the Pantry auto-remove setting).

Why can’t I see my partner’s changes?

Please check that:

  • you both have iCloud turned on (iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud),
  • you are both in the same household,
  • both iPhones are connected to the internet,
  • and pull down on the list to refresh.

iCloud sync usually takes a few seconds, sometimes a little longer.

How do I stop sharing or leave a household?

The household owner can stop sharing or remove an individual member at any time. A member can leave the household at any time.

What happens to my data when I leave a household?

Once you leave, you no longer see the household’s shared data (lists, history, Pantry and recurring items). The other members keep it.

Your warranties and settings were never shared, so you keep them.

Other

Which devices does Shoppie work on?

Shoppie is for iPhone with iOS 26 or later, including the foldable iPhone, and works in landscape too. It’s free.

Your data syncs through iCloud across all your iPhones.

Does Shoppie support VoiceOver and larger text?

Yes. Shoppie supports VoiceOver, larger text and Dark Mode. It’s available in Slovak, Czech and English and follows your iPhone’s language. To use Shoppie in a different language from the rest of your iPhone, go to iPhone Settings → Apps → Shoppie → Language.

How do I see the introduction again?

You’ll find an option to show the introduction again in the app’s settings.

How do I delete my data?

You have several options:

  • in the app: delete your shopping history or Pantry in settings; delete individual items and warranties directly in the app,
  • in iCloud: iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Shoppie,
  • by deleting the app from your iPhone (then remove the iCloud copy via Manage Storage).
