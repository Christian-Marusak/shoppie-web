Frequently asked questions

How do I add an item, and how does the wheel work?

Tap the green + button at the bottom and type what you want to buy.

Press and hold + to open a wheel with your favourite items. Pick one and it's on your list.

Why doesn't Shoppie suggest anything?

Suggestions come from your completed shopping trips. Shoppie needs a few of them to learn what you buy and how often.

Just shop as usual and complete each trip at the end. Suggestions will start to appear on their own.

How do I invite my family to my household, and what is shared?

You send the household invitation right from the app. Sharing works through iCloud, so the person you invite needs an iPhone signed in to an Apple Account with iCloud turned on.

Household members can see and edit lists, shopping history, best-before items and recurring items.

Warranties and settings are not shared – they stay personal.

A member can leave the household at any time, and the owner can remove members.

Why can't I see my partner's changes?

Please check that:

  • you both have iCloud turned on (iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud),
  • you are both in the same household,
  • both iPhones are connected to the internet,
  • and pull down on the list to refresh.

iCloud sync usually takes a few seconds, sometimes a little longer.

Why can't I scan receipts?

Receipt scanning reads the text right on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence, so it only works on iPhone models that support Apple Intelligence.

If your iPhone supports it, make sure Apple Intelligence is turned on in iPhone Settings (Apple Intelligence & Siri). All other Shoppie features work on any iPhone with iOS 26.

How do best-before reminders work, and why am I not getting them?

Each morning Shoppie reminds you about food that is close to its best-before date. The reminders are scheduled on your iPhone itself – nothing is sent from a server.

If they don't arrive:

  • allow notifications: iPhone Settings → Apps → Shoppie → Notifications,
  • check the reminder time in the app's Settings,
  • make sure the item has a best-before date,
  • and check that a Focus mode isn't hiding notifications.
How do I see the introduction again?

In the app, open Settings → Show Introduction.

How do I delete my data?

You have several options:

  • in the app's Settings: Delete Shopping History and Delete Best Before,
  • delete individual items in the app,
  • or everything at once: remove the app and its data from iCloud – iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Shoppie.

More in the privacy policy.

What happens to my data when I leave a household?

Once you leave a household, you no longer see its shared data (lists, history, best-before items and recurring items). The other members keep it.

Your warranties and settings were never shared, so you keep them.
