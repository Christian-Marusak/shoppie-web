How to scan a receipt on iPhone

Updated 26 November 2026 · 3 steps

In short

Tap the scan icon, take a photo of the receipt or pick it from your photos, and Shoppie recognises the products and prices, including discounts. The text is read on your iPhone, so it works offline and nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Step 1 Tap Scan Receipt

    On the List tab, tap the Scan Receipt icon at the top right (next to ⋯).

  2. Step 2 Photograph the receipt

    Choose Take Photo or Choose from Library. Flatten the receipt and photograph all of it.

  3. Step 3 Check and save

    Shoppie shows the store, date, total and items with prices. Check the names and tap the green ✓ (Save). The trip goes to History and the food to Pantry. Turn on Remove bought items from the list to clear your list at the same time.

Frequently asked questions

Does scanning work offline?

Yes. The receipt is read on your iPhone and nothing is uploaded. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple’s on-device model also helps with product names.

Why are some names abbreviated?

Receipts use abbreviations. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence some may stay as printed, so check them before saving.
