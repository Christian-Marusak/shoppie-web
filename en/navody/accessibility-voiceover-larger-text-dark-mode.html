Shoppie for everyone: VoiceOver, larger text and Dark Mode

Updated 26 November 2026 · 3 steps

In short

Shoppie supports VoiceOver, larger text and Dark Mode, and works in landscape too. It all follows your iPhone settings, so there’s nothing to set up in the app.

  1. Step 1 Larger text

    In iPhone Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size, make the text bigger. Shoppie follows it.

  2. Step 2 Dark Mode

    In iPhone Settings → Display & Brightness, choose Dark. Shoppie adapts.

  3. Step 3 VoiceOver

    In iPhone Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver, turn on the screen reader. Items, buttons and suggestions in Shoppie are all labelled.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shoppie work in landscape?

Yes, Shoppie works in landscape and supports the foldable iPhone too.

Why is Finish just an icon with larger text?

To leave room for your items. The ✓ icon does the same, and holding it shows the label.
