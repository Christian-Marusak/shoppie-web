How to add items fast with the favourites wheel

Updated 26 November 2026 · 3 steps

In short

Touch and hold the green + button, slide your finger to an item bubble and let go – it’s on your list. The wheel appears after your first finished trip, because it learns from your history. Choose what it offers in Settings → List → Quick add wheel.

  1. Step 1 Touch and hold +

    Instead of tapping, touch and hold the green + button. A wheel of bubbles appears around it.

  2. Step 2 Slide and let go

    Without lifting your finger, slide to an item bubble, for example bread rolls, and let go. The item is added straight away.

  3. Step 3 Choose what the wheel offers

    In Settings → List → Quick add wheel, choose Suggested, Recently bought or Most frequent.

Frequently asked questions

Why don’t I see the wheel?

The wheel learns from your shopping, so it appears once there’s at least one finished trip in History.
