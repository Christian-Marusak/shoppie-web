How to finish shopping and find it in your history

Updated 26 November 2026 · 3 steps

In short

When everything is in your basket, tap Finish. The trip is saved to History with the store and number of items. Swipe an item to the right to add it to your list again.

  1. Step 1 Tap Finish

    Once at least one item is ticked, Finish appears next to the + button. Tap it and confirm Finish Shopping. Ticked items are saved to History and removed from the list.

  2. Step 2 Open History

    The History tab shows all your trips with the date, store and number of items.

  3. Step 3 Add an item again

    Open a trip and swipe an item to the right → Add to List. To bring back the whole trip, use Add All to List.

Frequently asked questions

Why should I finish each trip?

Shoppie learns from finished trips what you buy and how often, and uses that to suggest what you’re running out of.
