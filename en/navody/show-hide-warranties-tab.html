How to show or hide the Warranties tab

Updated 26 November 2026 · 2 steps

In short

Show or hide the Warranties tab in Settings → Features → Warranties. It’s off by default. Turning it off deletes nothing – when you turn it back on, your warranties are still there.

  1. Step 1 Open Features

    On the Settings tab, find the Features section.

  2. Step 2 Toggle Warranties

    Use the Warranties switch to show or hide the tab. When it’s on, a Warranties tab appears at the bottom.

Frequently asked questions

Are my warranties deleted when I hide the tab?

No. Hiding the tab deletes nothing. Your warranties and reminders stay.
