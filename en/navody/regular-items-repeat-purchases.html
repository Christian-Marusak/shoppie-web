How to set up regular items

Updated 26 November 2026 · 3 steps

In short

For things you buy regularly, set how often you buy them. Shoppie then adds them to your shopping list on the chosen day.

  1. Step 1 Open Regular Items

    On the List tab, tap ⋯ at the top right and choose Regular Items. Or touch and hold an item on your list and choose Add Regularly.

  2. Step 2 Set how often

    Tap + (Add Regular Item), enter the item, turn on Repeat and set, for example, every 2 weeks (Days / Weeks / Months). Next time shows when it will be added.

  3. Step 3 Shoppie adds it for you

    On the chosen day, the item appears on your list. Swipe right to pause it.

Frequently asked questions

Are regular items added for my family too?

Yes. Regular items are shared with your household, so they appear on everyone’s list.

Can I turn the feature off?

Yes, in Settings → Features → Regular Items.
