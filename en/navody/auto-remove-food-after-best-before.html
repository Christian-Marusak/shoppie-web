How to turn on auto-remove for food past its date

Updated 26 November 2026 · 2 steps

In short

In Settings → Pantry → Remove from Pantry, choose how many days after the use-by date food should disappear on its own (after 1, 2, 3 or 7 days) and whether it counts as Used Up or Thrown Away. The default is Never, and the setting applies to the whole household.

  1. Step 1 Open Remove from Pantry

    On the Settings tab, under Pantry, tap Remove from Pantry and change Never to a number of days (after 1, 2, 3 or 7 days).

  2. Step 2 Choose how it counts

    In Count as, choose Used Up or Thrown Away. This row appears once you’ve picked a number of days.

Frequently asked questions

Does the setting apply to my family too?

Yes. Auto-remove applies to the whole household, and it works with estimated dates too.
