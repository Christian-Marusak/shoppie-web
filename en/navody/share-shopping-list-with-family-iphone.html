How to share a shopping list with your family on iPhone

Updated 26 November 2026 · 3 steps

In short

In Settings, under Sharing, tap Invite to Household and send the invitation, for example via Messages. Sharing works through iCloud, so everyone just needs an iPhone signed in to iCloud. Everything is shared except warranties and settings.

  1. Step 1 Open Sharing

    On the Settings tab, find the Sharing section. If you see a prompt to sign in, sign in to iCloud in iPhone Settings.

  2. Step 2 Send an invitation

    Tap Invite to Household and choose how to send it – for example Messages or Mail. They open it on their iPhone and join. Only the household owner can invite.

  3. Step 3 Check the members

    Household Members shows who has joined. Someone who hasn’t joined yet shows “Hasn’t accepted yet”. Add more people with Invite Someone Else.

Frequently asked questions

What is shared with the household?

Everything except warranties and settings – lists, shopping history, Pantry and regular items. Whoever joins brings their own lists too.

Does the person I invite need the app?

Yes – an iPhone with Shoppie, signed in to iCloud.
